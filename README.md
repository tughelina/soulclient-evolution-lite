# 🌱 SoulClient Evolution Stages · Lite

**See where your soul client stands inside, and what moves her to the next step.**

A method by **Chantal Perrinjaquet** (SoulStrategy · House Of Coaching), as a free skill for Claude.

---

## ✨ What it does for you

You describe your ideal client and your offers. The skill maps **one person on her inner path** in five stages: from *"I can do this myself"* to *"we carry it together"*.

You get one clear overview:

- 🧭 **Her coachability status** at every stage
- 🌊 **What changes inside her**
- 👀 **One signal you can actually observe**: in comments, DMs and purchases
- 🚪 **Your key window**: the moment she either allows guidance or buys elsewhere

---

## 🔍 Often confused with Eugene Schwartz. It is something else.

Eugene Schwartz's **5 Stages of Awareness** (unaware to most aware) sort people by **how close they are to buying a product**.

**SoulClient Evolution Stages sort one person by her coachability**: her readiness to let herself be precisely mirrored and guided, and in the end to carry the work further herself.

- A client can know your product perfectly and still not be coachable.
- Her revenue, age or profession never decide her stage. Her inner movement does.
- Every stage is a soul client. She simply buys at a different moment and needs different words.
- She may skip stages. A sensitive client often sees a later goal early.

## 🚀 How to use it

### Claude Code
Copy the folder `soulclient-evolution-lite/` into `~/.claude/skills/`, then type:

```
/soulclient-evolution-lite
```

### Claude.ai (Projects / Skills)
Upload the folder `soulclient-evolution-lite/` as a skill, then ask: *"Map the SoulClient Evolution Stages for my ideal client."*

Have ready: a short description of your ideal client and your offers.
