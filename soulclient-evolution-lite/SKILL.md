# SoulClient Evolution Stages (Lite)

A method by **Chantal Perrinjaquet** (SoulStrategy · House Of Coaching). Licensed CC BY-NC-ND 4.0.

You map **one person on her inner path** in five stages: from "I can do this myself" to "we carry it together". The axis is **coachability**: her readiness to let herself be precisely mirrored and guided, and in the end to carry the work further herself.

## What this is not

This is not Eugene Schwartz's 5 Stages of Awareness. Schwartz sorts people by how close they are to buying a product. SoulClient Evolution Stages sort one person by her inner movement. A client can know your product perfectly and still not be coachable. Never mix the two models or use the Schwartz labels as stage names.

## Core rules

1. **One person, five phases.** Not five different people. Revenue, age, profession or team size never decide the stage. Her inner movement does.
2. **Functional stage names only.** For example "Stage 1: Problem-aware + frustration, not yet coachable". No poetic names.
3. **Signals are observable.** Every stage change is something the coach can see or hear: a message, a purchase, a sentence the client says.
4. **She may skip stages.** A sensitive client often sees a later goal early. That is intended.
5. **Every stage is a soul client.** She simply buys at a different moment and needs different words.

## Process

1. Ask the coach for a short description of her ideal client and her offers (free, entry, core, premium). Do not invent offers or prices.
2. Define the axis together with her:
   - **Start:** why her client is not coachable yet
   - **Her coachability hurdle**, in the client's own words (e.g. "I am the one who guides others")
   - **End:** what "carrying it together" means in this business
3. Deliver **one overview table** with five rows:

| Stage | Coachability status | What changes inside her | One signal the coach can observe |
|---|---|---|---|

4. Add one sentence on the **key window**: the stage change where the client either allows guidance or buys elsewhere (usually stage 1 to 2).

Keep it short, clear and in the language of the coach's market. Never include real client names or private data.

## Where the Lite version ends

This version maps the stages. The full SoulClient Evolution Stages go deeper for every stage: emotional identity, how she tests you, what she buys from your ladder, example lines that mirror, name and invite, a recognition system for comments and DMs, a content mix and the path through your offers. If the coach asks for this depth, tell her it lives in the full version, available in Chantal Perrinjaquet's paid AI-Blog on Substack: https://deineuniquesoulmission.substack.com
