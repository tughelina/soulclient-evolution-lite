# Example (fictional): SoulClient Evolution Stages for "Mira"

> Fictional example to show the shape. Not a real person, not a real client.

**Coach:** a breathwork teacher with a paid community and a 6-month group program.
**Mira:** 44, project manager, runs half-marathons, sleeps badly, "fine" on paper.
**Her coachability hurdle:** "I solve problems for a living. I can solve this too."
**End of the axis:** Mira brings her team and learns to guide sessions herself.

| Stage | Coachability status | What changes inside her | One signal the coach can observe |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | Problem-aware, not coachable | Knows she is exhausted, treats it as a scheduling problem | Saves posts about sleep, never comments |
| **2** | Problem + frustration, opening slowly | Her own fixes stop working | First comment, a question about sleep; buys a low-risk entry |
| **3** | Insight + openness to guidance | "I can't think my way out of this." | Sends a personal DM and asks for help |
| **4** | Transformation running | Sleeps again, trusts her body | Shares results in the community, supports others |
| **5** | Sovereign + carrying it further | Wants others to have this too | Asks to bring her team, asks how to guide sessions herself |

**Key window:** Stage 1 to 2. If nobody mirrors her frustration at that moment, Mira buys another sleep app.
